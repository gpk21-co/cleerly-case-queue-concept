The problem I was thinking about

Cleerly's public materials describe a shift in what's slowing clinical teams down: coverage for AI-QCT plaque analysis has expanded significantly through Medicare and major payers, so the friction is moving from "will this be reimbursed" to "can staff work this into their day without administrative drag." Cleerly PREVIEW addresses this directly by surfacing stenosis values and reimbursement-threshold mapping earlier in the workflow.

This prototype is my own attempt to sketch what a case-queue view built around that idea might look like: severity drives sort order, and clicking into a case shows exactly where it lands against reimbursement thresholds — the same logic I understand PREVIEW to apply, rebuilt independently with placeholder data as a product-thinking exercise.
